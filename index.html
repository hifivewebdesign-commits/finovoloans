Answers to Common Borrower Doubts

Transparency is our first commitment. Here are answers to common questions customers ask before applying.

FINOVO LOANS assists customers by connecting them with suitable lending institutions, including banks and NBFCs. Loan approval and disbursal are handled by the respective lender.

Never pay an individual who promises guaranteed loan approval. Any legitimate applicable charges must be explained and verified before payment. Legitimate bank processing fees are deducted directly from loan disbursement in most cases.

You can contact our team to discuss your situation. Available options depend on the lender's policies and your overall financial profile. Approval cannot be guaranteed.
