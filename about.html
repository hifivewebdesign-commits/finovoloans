Borrower Protection & Ethics

What Makes FINOVO LOANS Different

We pride ourselves on full disclosure so you can make informed, secure financial decisions with absolute confidence.

What FINOVO LOANS Does

  • Assesses your income, turnover, and CIBIL to recommend suitable banks & NBFCs.
  • Coordinates paperwork, legal verification, and property valuation liaison.
  • Transparently informs you of any consultancy charges before proceeding.
  • Assists with multiple loan products (Business, Personal, Car, Mortgage, OD & CC).
  • Ensures data confidentiality under strict privacy guidelines.

What We Never Do

  • We NEVER promise 100% "guaranteed approval" — approvals rest solely with the lender.
  • We NEVER collect upfront advance fees promising guaranteed sanction letters.
  • We NEVER initiate unwarranted credit enquiries that harm your CIBIL score.
  • We NEVER lend money directly; all disbursals originate from RBI-regulated entities.
  • We NEVER share your confidential records with unauthorized third parties.
