Working Capital Credit

OD & CC Facilities (Overdraft & Cash Credit)

Overdraft (OD) and Cash Credit (CC) limits are dynamic revolving credit solutions designed to empower businesses, wholesalers, manufacturers, and contractors to bridge cash flow gaps and manage fluctuating operations without taking term loans.

Understanding OD vs CC:

OD (Overdraft)

Allows account holders to withdraw beyond available balance up to an agreed sanction limit. Backed by financial records, fixed deposits, or collateral.

CC (Cash Credit)

Working capital limit primarily determined by the borrower's drawing power calculated from current stock, inventory, and book debts/receivables.

Key Advantages:

  • Interest on Utilized Sum Only: You do not pay interest on the full sanctioned limit — only on the actual amount utilized daily.
  • Limit Enhancements: We assist operating businesses with growing turnover in enhancing their existing OD/CC limits.
  • Annual Renewal: Facilities are renewed annually based on satisfactory account turnover and business health.
OD & CC FAQs

Eligibility & Documents

Eligible Applicants:

Traders, wholesalers, distributors, manufacturers, contractors, and service companies with regular current account transactions.

Required Documents:
  • Audited Financial Statements (Last 2 to 3 years)
  • 12 Months current account bank statements
  • GST returns (GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B for 12 months)
  • Latest monthly stock and book debt statement
  • List of major buyers, suppliers, and debtor ageing report
  • Existing sanction letters for takeover / limit enhancement
